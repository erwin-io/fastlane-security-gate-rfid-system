Gate Motor, Solenoid & Buzzer

Enable TB6600 gate motor
Invert TB6600 direction
Enable buzzer feedback
Solenoid behavior: Relay polarity is fixed ACTIVE-HIGH for fail-safe startup: GPIO15 LOW = LOCKED/OFF, GPIO15 HIGH = UNLOCKED/ON. On GRANTED the relay unlocks immediately with no pre-motor delay. Default pulse = 1000 ms. After the pulse it returns to LOCK even while the barrier is still open or moving. The return motor does not wait for or control the relay; the mechanical lock is expected to slide/re-catch automatically when the barrier returns to the Closed Angle. The controller still assumes the physical gate starts at the Closed Angle because no home sensor is installed yet.
